How It Works

From a single leaf photo to a detailed disease report with remedial solutions — in under two seconds.

Step 01
📷
Upload Leaf Photo

Drag & drop or click to upload any JPG/PNG image of a tomato leaf. Works on phone photos too.

Step 02
🧠
CBAM Attention

Channel and spatial attention gates highlight the exact disease regions, filtering out background noise.

Step 03
⚡
EfficientNet-B0

A compound-scaled CNN extracts 1,280-dimensional feature vectors from the attention-weighted regions.

Step 04
📊
Diagnosis & Plan

Receive disease classification, confidence scores, Grad-CAM heatmap, and a full remedial action plan.

          Model Variants

          Three attention variants tested under identical conditions — same data, same hyperparameters.

          Baseline
          EfficientNet-B0
          99.23%

          Standard pretrained EfficientNet-B0 with a custom classifier head. No attention module — serves as the control.

          SE Net
          Squeeze-Excitation
          99.34%

          Adds channel-wise recalibration (squeeze-and-excitation) after the backbone. Learns which feature maps to emphasise.

          🏆 Best
          CBAM
          99.38%

          Convolutional Block Attention Module — sequential channel and spatial attention. Achieves the highest accuracy and F1.

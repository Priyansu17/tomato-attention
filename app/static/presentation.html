Challenge & Motivation

The Agricultural Crisis: Foliar Pathogens

Tomato crops suffer massive economic and food-security losses due to rapid disease propagation.

📉
Devastating Yield Destruction: Pathogens such as Late Blight (Phytophthora infestans) and Bacterial Spot can eradicate entire open-field yields within 48 to 72 hours under warm, humid conditions.
🔍
Limitations of Manual Scouting: Visual inspection is labor-intensive, subject to human subjectivity, and practically inaccessible to smallholder farmers lacking on-site agronomists.
🛑
The "Black-Box" Neural Network Trap: Standard deep CNNs frequently focus on background soil, greenhouse pipes, or lighting artifacts instead of the actual lesion pathology, triggering dangerous false positives.
💡
Our Paradigm: An explainable, attention-augmented vision network that explicitly validates leaf anatomy, focuses on necrotic tissue, and generates immediate actionable remediation.

Backbone Architecture

Foundation: Pretrained EfficientNet-B0

Compound coefficient scaling achieving state-of-the-art representation with mobile-ready parameters.

📐
Principled Compound Scaling: Balances network depth ($d=\alpha^\phi$), channel width ($w=\beta^\phi$), and input resolution ($r=\gamma^\phi$) uniformly under a constant computational budget $\phi$.
⚡
Mobile Inverted Bottleneck (MBConv): Utilizes depthwise separable convolutions with inverted residual shortcuts and internal squeeze-and-excitation blocks for minimal FLOP overhead.
🪶
Extreme Parameter Efficiency: Only 5.3 Million Parameters — roughly 5× smaller than ResNet-50 (25.6M) and 26× lighter than VGG-16 (138M) with superior top-1 feature extraction.
🎯
Pretrained Transfer Weights: Pretrained on ImageNet-1K, providing generalized multi-scale edge, texture, and chlorosis filters prior to task-specific agronomy fine-tuning.
Core Innovation

CBAM: Dual Channel & Spatial Attention

Sequential decomposition: learning "what" features to emphasize and "where" lesions are located.

📡
Channel Attention Module (What to focus on): Exploits both Global Average Pooling (GAP) and Global Max Pooling (GMP) through a shared two-layer MLP with reduction ratio $r=16$:
M_c(F) = σ( MLP(AvgPool(F)) + MLP(MaxPool(F)) )
🗺️
Spatial Attention Module (Where to look): Pools channel activations across space and passes the concatenated maps through a large $7\times 7$ convolution:
M_s(F') = σ( f^{7×7}( [AvgPool(F'); MaxPool(F')] ) )
🔬
Feature Recalibration: Intermediate feature maps $\mathbf{F} \in \mathbb{R}^{C \times H \times W}$ are sequentially scaled: $\mathbf{F}' = \mathbf{M}_c(\mathbf{F}) \otimes \mathbf{F}$, followed by $\mathbf{F}'' = \mathbf{M}_s(\mathbf{F}') \otimes \mathbf{F}'$.
🎯
Noise Suppression: Irrelevant spatial noise (background trays, specular leaf glare, soil) is systematically dampened while concentric target-rings and necrotic margins are highlighted.
Ablation Study

Architectural Variants Compared

Rigorous benchmark comparing Baseline, Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE), and Dual CBAM.

⚪
Variant A: Baseline EfficientNet-B0: Raw backbone with standard Global Average Pooling directly into the Dense classifier. Good baseline, but lacks selective spatial lesion weighting.
🟡
Variant B: Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE): Adds 1D channel attention recalibration. Models cross-channel interdependencies, but remains spatially agnostic across the $7\times 7$ grid.
🟢
Variant C: Dual CBAM (Our Champion): Cascades channel recalibration with spatial convolution. Accurately pinpoints isolated fungal spots and multi-focal bacterial lesions simultaneously.
⚡
Parameter Efficiency: CBAM introduces <0.2% Additional Parameters over baseline, maintaining real-time inference latency (42ms on GPU, 1.2s on CPU).
Training Protocol

Two-Phase Training & Optimization

Differential learning rates, warmup, and modern regularization preventing catastrophic forgetting.

❄️
Phase A: Frozen Backbone Warmup (2 Epochs): Pretrained ImageNet weights are frozen; only the newly added CBAM module and dense classification head are trained at $\text{LR} = 10^{-4}$ to stabilize random gradients.
🔥
Phase B: Differential Fine-Tuning (38 Epochs): The entire backbone is unfrozen with differential learning rates: backbone at conservative $\text{LR} = 10^{-5}$ and classifier head at $\text{LR} = 10^{-4}$.
📉
Cosine Annealing Schedule: Dynamic decay down to $\eta_{\text{min}} = 10^{-6}$ with 3-epoch linear warmup, guiding the optimizer toward flat, generalized minima.
🛡️
Label Smoothing & Mixed Precision: Applied Label Smoothing ($\lambda = 0.1$) to prevent overconfident boundary over-fitting, alongside AMP FP16 acceleration and AdamW weight decay ($10^{-4}$).
Interpretability

Explainable AI via Grad-CAM Saliency

Validating that neural predictions are driven by authentic pathological evidence.

🗺️
Gradient-Weighted Class Activation Maps: Calculates gradients of the predicted class score $y^c$ with respect to the final convolutional feature maps $A^k$, deriving importance weights $\alpha_k^c$:
L^c_{\text{Grad-CAM}} = \text{ReLU}\left(\sum_k \alpha_k^c A^k\right)
📍
Post-Attention Hooking: Hooks directly into the CBAM output layer, visualizing how spatial attention dynamically concentrates gradient mass on diseased leaf tissues.
🔬
Pathological Correlation: Confirmed that high saliency (red/amber heat) precisely overlaps physical fungal spore centers, bacterial water-soaked lesions, and viral chlorotic curling.
🤝
Building Agronomist Trust: Eliminates the "black-box" skepticism by giving field farmers and plant pathologists verifiable visual proof for every diagnosis.
Production Safety

4-Tier Out-of-Distribution Guardrails

Multi-layer computer vision shielding against non-plant, non-tomato, and out-of-context uploads.

🌿
Tier 1 — Foliage & Chlorophyll Guard: Analyzes HSV green-chrominance spectrum and leaf contour area. Instantly rejects arbitrary images (faces, cars, documents, animals, blank frames).
🏛️
Tier 1.5 — Studio & Mortar Context Guard: Rejects samples photographed inside lab mortars, bowls, or high-contrast reflective tables that induce distribution shift.
🤖
Tier 1.6 — Zero-Shot CLIP Species Guard: Employs OpenAI's CLIP ViT-B/32 vision-language embeddings to verify species identity — rejecting non-tomato leaves (potato, apple, grapevine, weeds).
📊
Tier 2 — Softmax Entropy & Confidence Filter: Computes Shannon entropy on output logits; flags ambiguous samples ($H > 1.8$ or $\text{conf} < 0.60$) for manual extension inspection.
